Ever had a client question an invoice, or wonder if a driver actually made it to a job site as planned? Your AccuGPS dashboard has a tool built for exactly that situation: Trip Replay.

Trip Replay lets you review the full route history for any vehicle in your fleet. You can see every stop a vehicle made, the exact route it took, its speed at any point, and how long it spent at each location.

This feature is useful for:

* Verifying service calls for billing * Resolving client disputes about arrival or departure times * Auditing driver activity and routes

To use Trip Replay:

1. Log in to your AccuGPS dashboard. 2. Select the "Trip Replay" module from the navigation menu. 3. Choose the vehicle and date you want to review. 4. Press play to watch the trip unfold on the map, or scroll through the detailed timeline.

This can save you time and prevent lost revenue by giving you clear, undeniable proof of service.
